About

Designer who thinks in systems. Artist who works in dreams.

I'm Cleanwell Mwalwanda, a computer science graduate and graphic designer working at the intersection of logic and imagination. My practice spans brand identity, surreal digital art, and visual design systems.

I believe the best design doesn't announce itself. It creates conditions for feeling: a mark that stays, a composition that unsettles, an identity that holds. Whether building a brand or compositing a surreal world, I'm always looking for the thing that makes it true.
